# AI Policy

This policy governs the use of AI assistants in Evochora. It binds every person who works on the project, without exception, and thereby states what the project itself adheres to.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to every contribution to Evochora and to every research output the project publishes.

- A **contribution** is anything submitted to the project's repository or its issue tracker, including commits, pull requests, issues, comments and reviews.
- A **research output** is a work published for citation, such as a dataset, an experiment record or a paper.
- An **AI assistant** is a system that generates content from instructions. A tool that only corrects spelling or grammar is not an AI assistant.

## 2. Permitted Use

AI assistants may be used for any contribution and any research output, provided that this policy is complied with. Work that does not comply is not accepted into the project and is not published by it.

## 3. Authorship and Responsibility

Only a person is an author. An AI assistant may be used only on the instruction of a person, and that person is responsible for the result as for their own work: for its correctness, for its originality and for the right to publish it under the applicable license. Every decision in the project, including every decision on a contribution or in a review, must be made by a person.

## 4. Review

A person who submits work made with an AI assistant must have reviewed it, must understand it and must be able to account for it. The work must meet the same standards as any other work.

## 5. Disclosure

Work made with an AI assistant must disclose that fact.

- A contribution must name the assistant, preferably in a trailer line `Assisted-by: <assistant>`. A trailer line that names an AI assistant, in whatever form, denotes assistance and never authorship.
- A research output must state within the work itself that AI assistants were used, for what purpose and under whose responsibility, as the standards of scholarly publishing require, and must refer to this policy.
- The project discloses its use of AI assistants as a whole in its README.

## 6. Effective Date

This policy takes effect on 12 November 2026. AI assistants have been used in Evochora since its inception. Work that predates this policy may have been made with their assistance, whether or not it is marked accordingly.
