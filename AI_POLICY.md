# AI Policy

This policy sets the rules for working with AI assistants in Evochora. It applies to everyone who works in the project with such tools.

## Use

AI assistants may be used for work of every kind, code, documents, analyses, issues and reviews, under the requirements below and not otherwise.

## Requirements

- **Disclosure.** Everything written with AI assistance says so; spelling and grammar tools need no mention. What belongs to the development process, a commit, a pull request, an issue, a comment, says it with a trailer line, `Co-Authored-By: <assistant>` or `Assisted-by: <assistant>`. What the project publishes as a document says it with this note:

  > Written with AI assistance under the project's [AI Policy](AI_POLICY.md). The author reviewed it and is responsible for its content.

- **Understanding.** Every contribution made with an assistant is read and understood before it is contributed, and the one who contributes it can explain it. It meets the same standards as any other contribution, in tests, style and review.

- **Responsibility.** An assistant acts only on the instruction of a person, and that person is responsible for everything the assistant does and produces: for its content, its correctness and its right to be contributed under the project's license. A decision in the project, on a contribution or on a review, is a person's decision.

A contribution that does not meet these requirements is not accepted.

## Since when

This policy is effective from 8 November 2026. Documents created or revised after that date carry the note above. AI assistants have been used in Evochora since its beginning, and this policy discloses that use for everything made before it.
